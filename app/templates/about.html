About GoShow

Concert booking made simple

GoShow is a concert booking website for finding live music and reserving tickets in one place. It is built to keep the main booking steps clear and easy to use.

Visitors can browse concerts, open the details page for a show, book tickets, and manage their bookings afterward. The admin page is available for managing the concert list used by the website.
